# Variant: Dark Museum ("Cabinet of Curiosities")

## Design stance
The collector as curator: near-black gallery walls, gold-leaf accents,
Cormorant serif, framed photographs hung salon-style with museum label
plaques. Premium, nocturnal, deliberately theatrical.

## Key choices
- Layout: editorial hero with a framed "wall" of five tilted collector photos
  (vinyl centrepiece + sneaker/comic/figure/camera corners), plinth base,
  roman-numeral premium "vault"
- Typography: Cormorant Garamond display (italic gold accents) + Inter UI,
  letterspaced uppercase kickers
- Color: warm black `#12100c`, gold `#c8a04a`, muted parchment ink
- Interaction: drifting frames, hover-scaled frames revealing label plaques,
  discipline marquee, scroll-reveal, medallion counters
- Imagery: framed real photos with museum captions ("The Sunday Shelf — 214 LPs")

## Trade-offs
- Strong at: maximum creative flair, mood, memorability; positions Premium as
  "the vault" — fits collectd's value ladder
- Weak at: darkest and least conventional for a public landing; needs real
  photography to land in production; light-theme parity TBD

## Best for
- The "show me something with soul" answer — great as a seasonal/dark-mode
  identity or for a premium/launch splash rather than the always-on default.
