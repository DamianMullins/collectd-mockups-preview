Field notes

Asked & answered

What can I collect here?+
Anything with catalogue logic — cards, comics, sneakers, vinyl, cameras, LEGO, coins, figures. Fields are fully customisable per collection.
Are my collections private?+
New collections start private. Publishing is deliberate and reversible, and private collections never appear in search or discover.
How does copying work?+
Copy any public collection to get its field structure, then make it yours. Lineage is tracked so creators are credited.
What if I cancel Premium?+
Nothing is deleted. You return to free limits and lose Premium-only themes — items, images and fields stay intact.
