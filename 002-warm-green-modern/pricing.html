FAQ

Pricing questions

Is there really a free plan?+
Yes — genuinely free, forever. 3 collections, 100 items each, public galleries and grid/table views. No trial countdown, no card required. Upgrade only when you need the room.
What happens if I cancel Pro?+
Nothing is deleted. You keep every collection, item, image and field — you simply drop back to free limits and lose Pro-only features like custom themes, analytics and private collections. Export is always available.
Can I pay annually for a discount?+
Yes. Annual billing is $40/year — about $3.33 a month — which works out ~33% cheaper than monthly. Toggle the billing period above to compare.
What is Lifetime Access?+
A one-time $99 payment for all Pro features with no subscription. Perfect if you've found your forever home and never want to think about the bill again.
How do refunds work?+
New Pro subscriptions are covered by our 14-day money-back guarantee — no questions asked. Lifetime Access is refundable within the same window. See the full Refund Policy for details.
