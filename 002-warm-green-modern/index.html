FAQ

Questions, answered

What can I collect on collectd?+
Anything with a catalogue logic. Trading cards, comics, sneakers, vinyl, cameras, LEGO, coins, action figures, minerals — if you can list it, you can build a collection for it. The fields are fully customisable per collection.
Are my collections private by default?+
Yes. New collections start private and only you can see them. Publishing a gallery is a deliberate, reversible choice — and unpublished collections never appear in search or discover.
How does copying another collection work?+
On any public collection, hit Copy. You get the field structure (and public items where the owner allows), then you adapt and fill it with your own data. Lineage is tracked so creators are credited for templates others reuse.
What happens to my data if I cancel Premium?+
Nothing is deleted. You drop back to free limits and lose Premium-only themes — but every item, image and field stays intact. Export is available any time, even on free.
