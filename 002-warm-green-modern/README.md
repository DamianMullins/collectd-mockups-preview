# Variant: Warm Green Modern

## Design stance
Keep collectd's own green identity, borrow Settee's *craft*: warm paper
background, serif display type (Newsreader), image-led product mockups —
the realistic evolution path rather than a rebrand.

## Key choices
- Layout: centred hero statement with a full browser-frame product mockup
  (a live gallery page), floating stat chips, feature grid, alternating splits
- Typography: Newsreader serif display with green italic accents + Inter UI
- Color: collectd greens (`#2e6b3f` / `#429a53`) on warm `#f5f2ea` paper
- Interaction: floating chips, spin-ring decoration, hover lifts, FAQ
  accordions, scroll-reveal, stat-band
- Imagery: real photos inside a mocked-up "The Sunday Shelf" gallery page —
  shows the product, not just the brand

## Trade-offs
- Strong at: showing what today's collectd becomes with warmth + editorial
  polish; safest of the three vs the current brand
- Weak at: less of a *wow* departure than the other two; browser mockup pushes
  the first big visual below the fold

## Best for
- The likely shippable direction — green stays, everything gets warmer,
  more human, more image-led.
