Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about collectd.

What is collectd?+
Collectd is a community platform for collectors. Build custom collections for any hobby — trading cards, comic books, sneakers, vinyl, and more. Track your inventory with flexible fields, share beautiful public galleries, and discover what others are collecting.
What can I collect?+
Anything! Collectd uses a flexible field system so you can create custom collection templates for any collectible. Popular categories include trading cards, comic books, sneakers, vinyl records, Funko Pops, and more.
Is it free?+
Collectd offers a generous free tier that lets you create collections, add items, and share public galleries. Pro plans unlock advanced features like data export/import, analytics, and gallery theming.
Can I import my existing collection?+
Yes! Pro users can import items via CSV or JSON files. You can also export your collections at any time in either format.
How do public galleries work?+
Every collection can be published as a public gallery with a shareable link. Visitors can browse your items, filter and search, and star collections they like. You can customize gallery layouts and themes with a Pro plan.
