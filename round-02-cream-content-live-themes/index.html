FAQ

Before you ask

What can I actually collect on collectd?+
Anything with a catalogue logic. The platform is deliberately generic — trading cards, comics, sneakers, vinyl, cameras, action figures, coins, minerals, tickets, LEGO sets. If you can list it, you can build a collection for it.
Is my collection private by default?+
Yes. New collections start private — you choose when to publish a gallery. Even published, items stay under your control and you can unpublish or delete at any time.
What does “copy a collection” mean?+
Any public collection can be copied as a template. You get the field structure (and public items where the owner allows), then you fill it with your own data. The original creator keeps credit through lineage tracking.
Do I need Premium to share galleries?+
No. Public galleries with grid and table views are free. Showcase layout, custom accent colours and cover images are Premium perks.
