Thirteen ways to make the page play

Every idea from the brainstorm, each in its own section — hover, click, scroll and press keys. Built on the live light/dark tokens, mobile-friendly, and it all stands down for prefers-reduced-motion. Pick the numbers you like and they go into the homepage mock.

Spinning dashed ring

The green-mock circle, recoloured to the theme primary. Slow, calm rotation that picks up speed on hover. Sits behind the card — we learnt that lesson.

Rotating text stamp

A circular wax-seal label orbiting a photo — "COLLECT • TRACK • SHARE • COPY" on a text path, spinning like a record. Very vinyl-hero.

Record-player hero

A vinyl disc spins with a moving sheen; hover the sleeve and it lifts off the record. Peak collectible energy for the vinyl crowd.
